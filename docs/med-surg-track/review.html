  • Strong requires independent Accepted evidence on every expected mapped decision across C1/C2/C3. A2 needs D3, help and report; A3 needs action, concern and findings. Accepted with a note does not earn Strong.
  • One practice card: among independent Revisit, Outside role or Unsafe choices, prioritize any Unsafe first, then C3, C1, C2. Assisted wrong responses do not create a weakness claim. Hints alone never create a practice recommendation.
  • Partial independent positives produce What we saw with only the observations actually supported. No mapped unaided evidence means no card. Missing evidence implies neither success nor failure.
  • Cards are frozen from the original run when first completed. Guided retries and Rewind remain separately identified and never replace originals.
  • After A3, choose the most recent earlier assignment with a practice card, otherwise A2. Use original first responses and disclose assistance. No same-component usable independent evidence means no change claim.
  • For C3 help/timing, compare the earlier original help/action with A3’s locked action. If the practice focus was the report itself, show that report and explain A3 does not separately test spoken reporting; no improvement claim.
  • A2 C1/C2 are coverage/action proxies. Comparing those with A3 linked findings/hypotheses is explicitly different-format and cannot establish improvement in those components.
  • Only comparable independent choices can support improvement, consistency, continued difficulty or current-needs-review. Even supported improvement concerns these recorded choices, not clinical competence, validated transfer or an effect caused by CE.
  • Completing Rewind is descriptive context, never a causal explanation. Everyone receives A1→A2→A3; the next case is not dynamically generated or selected.

Sources, scope assumptions and assets

oximetry / FDA: Pulse Oximeters

Pulse oximetry estimates oxygen saturation and should be interpreted with symptoms. It does not validate a reassuring saturation as a reason to dismiss a change in breathing or alertness.

cjmm / OpenStax: Clinical Judgment Measurement Model overview

Educational framework for recognising and analysing cues and taking action. This mapping is an authored interpretation, not an endorsed or validated assessment.

cdc / CDC: About Sepsis

General symptom constellation and need for evaluation. Does not diagnose the fictional patient or validate the response rules.

process / CDC: Caring for Patients with Sepsis

Prompt evaluation and use of the facility’s policies and procedures. No treatment orders or national bedside response threshold are inferred.

nice / NICE NG253: Could this be sepsis?

Changes from usual behaviour and circulation or respiration, including presentations without high temperature. UK guidance supports teaching principles; it is not a US hospital protocol.

shock / OpenStax: Hypovolemic Shock

Concern about a postoperative vital-sign trend; no diagnosis of bleeding is established by the limited initial observations.

rrt / AHRQ PSNet: Rapid Response Systems

Escalation principles, with local criteria and process. No medication, oxygen or device changes are instructed.
