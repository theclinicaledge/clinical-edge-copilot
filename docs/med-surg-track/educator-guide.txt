PURPOSE AND SEQUENCE
Everyone completes The Drowsy Patient, Which Patient First?, then Afternoon
Handoff. The cases are fictional adult inpatient med-surg situations. Practice
suggestions and the last comparison respond to the learner's original choices;
the assignments themselves are fixed. No certification, clinical competence or
causal learning-effect inference is supported.

BEFORE A REVIEW SESSION
Open review.html for the exact cases, response matrix, rationale, source scope
and unresolved questions. Review the borderline A1 assessment branch, the A2
wound description, A3 acute hypotheses and urgency, and the assistance mapping.
Use a fresh test browser/profile when an independent walkthrough is desired.
The application deliberately does not import standalone Shift or prior Replay
history as independent track evidence. Do not enter real patient information.

DURING THE EXPERIENCE
Let the nurse inspect the available information and commit without coaching.
In Afternoon Handoff, the chart and bedside can be opened in any order or not
opened. An action locks before the concern and linked-finding options appear.
Do not preview its target skill, guided comparison or answer key. Teaching and
up to two bedside-check results follow the complete response.

The debrief offers at most one useful takeaway. Rewind opens over the debrief;
it is guided practice with three complete authored situations. A submitted
variation records practice. Closing without submitting records only a visit.
The learner may always close it and return. Escalation is not always accepted.
Information gathering requires a specific item. The sheet includes accepted
alternatives and scope after submission.

HOW TO READ THE RECORD
Strong: every mapped decision across the three components was independently
Accepted on the first attempt. This is an observation about a bounded exercise.
What we saw: only the supported observations with independent evidence.
Worth practicing: an independent original Revisit, Outside role or Unsafe
decision, prioritizing Unsafe, then action/help/report, noticing, reassurance.
No card: no adequate independent mapped evidence. Missing or assisted evidence
is neither failure nor success. A hint by itself never indicates difficulty.

Important source limitation: A1's existing priority feedback already identifies
the alertness cue; cue teaching then precedes the action and SBAR. All its mapped
choices are conservatively assisted. A1 therefore produces no performance card
in this unchanged sequence. It still offers optional Rewind and the next case.
A2 has substantial prompt scaffolding: its C1/C2 mapping is a coverage/action
proxy, and the help step already prompts a call. Read all claims narrowly.

The final comparison uses the most recent earlier original response that
prompted relevant practice, otherwise the relevant A2 response. It never uses
a coached retry as a new first response. A2 coverage proxies cannot establish
improvement in A3 cue interpretation. A report-specific difficulty cannot be
declared improved by a different help action: A3 does not test a spoken report.
Rewind's call exercise also does not assess free SBAR composition. A comparison
may appropriately say that independent change cannot be established.

AFTERWARD
Ask what led to the action and whether the explanation fits the whole picture.
Discuss plausible acute alternatives without requiring a single diagnosis.
Use the source/rationale disclosure and hospital-process assumptions. Do not
substitute the pilot's labels for clinical judgment or hospital protocols.

The learner can open Local record and privacy to export a JSON record or
deliberately clear this track. Export is optional and shares nothing by itself.
No educator dashboard, server record or automatic collection exists. Review a
learner's export only if they choose to share it through an agreed process.

LOCAL RECORD LIMITS
All runs, original decisions, assistance history and guided practice remain on
one browser/device. A repeated assignment is guided; originals and frozen cards
remain unchanged. Unknown content/mapping/source versions or unreadable saved
data are preserved for export rather than rescored or silently replaced.
If storage fails, the page warns that new in-memory progress may be lost on
refresh. Export before leaving. A single active tab is the supported use;
concurrent writing tabs have no conflict-resolution guarantee. Browser data
clearing or private-browsing policies may remove records. There is no sync.

BEFORE ANY LEARNER RELEASE
Complete the review record in the packet, resolve authoring questions, rerun
verification after approved content changes, and complete physical iPhone
checks in validation.txt. Founder release authorization is still required.
Do not treat source checks, automated tests or screenshots as clinical review.
